# RULEBREAK — Store Listing Draft

## Product positioning
**RULEBREAK** is a short-form puzzle game built around one idea: learn the rule, then notice when the game asks you to break it.

## Title
RULEBREAK

## Short description
Learn the rule. Spot the twist. Break it before time runs out.

## Full description
RULEBREAK turns tiny logic puzzles into fast tests of attention, memory and reaction.

Every challenge teaches you a simple rule. Then the game changes something: a signal, pattern, instruction, timing, position or obvious answer. Your job is to notice the change and respond correctly.

### Why it feels different
- Short challenges designed for quick sessions.
- Six puzzle families: SEE, REMEMBER, REACT, SWITCH, TRICK and MIX.
- Immediate visual, haptic and audio feedback.
- Progress is stored locally on the device.
- Designed to work offline.
- No account or online service is required for the MVP.

### The core loop
1. Read the rule.
2. Watch the challenge.
3. Spot what changed.
4. Choose the answer.
5. Learn from the feedback.
6. Keep your streak alive.

RULEBREAK is deliberately simple to start and difficult to master. The challenge is not memorizing a complicated rulebook — it is noticing the moment when the obvious answer stops being correct.
