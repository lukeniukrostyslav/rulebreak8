## One-minute reconstruction
RULEBREAK is an Android-first premium micro-puzzle game. The player learns a simple rule and then has to detect when the rule changes or when the obvious interpretation is wrong. Rounds are short (roughly 5–30 seconds). Target price is about €1.99 on Google Play. MVP is offline-first with no ads, subscriptions, IAP, backend, or AI runtime dependency.
